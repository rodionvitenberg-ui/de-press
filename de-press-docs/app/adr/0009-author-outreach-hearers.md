# Author Outreach to Hearers

Silent Empathy creates Hearers. The **public** never sees who heard; the **author** may see a private Hearer List and start Initiated Dialogue with one Hearer, several, or a random opt-in Hearer (Author Outreach). This counters the “graveyard of monologues” without public reactions: the author can reach warmth with low energy (e.g. “write to someone who heard”). Hearers get outreach consent (default on with opt-out). Email and real identity stay hidden.
