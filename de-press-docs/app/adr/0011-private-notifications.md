# Private Notifications on Redis (Channel layer), not public vanity

Author-private nudges are delivered through a **Notification** row (source of truth with read state) plus a broadcast to a per-recipient Channel layer group (`notify.account.{id}` / `notify.session.{id}`). The frontend receives them via a global `ws/notifications/` socket registered in the Anti-Panic killer, with an HTTP polling fallback. Email is intentionally deferred (P1 scope is in-app only): no SMTP config, no Celery email task yet. Notifications are never shown publicly — no counters of who “heard”, no engagement vanity. The in-app bell does not list Support Clouds or chat messages (feed-row gesture and chat-list unread instead; ADR 0017). Read state (model `Notification.is_read`) is the source for future ethical metrics (e.g. authors who opened dialogues), not for public popularity.
