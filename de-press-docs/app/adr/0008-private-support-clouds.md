# Private Support Clouds, not public comments

Support replies under a Story are **Support Clouds**: never a public comment thread or popularity contest. Listeners may send **Quiet Phrases** (curated templates, zero moderation, instant delivery) or short free-text **Moderated Clouds** (manual review before delivery). Long-form peer support still goes through DialogueRequest / Initiated Dialogue.

**Status:** accepted for the data model. Author-facing UI is superseded by [ADR 0017](./0017-clouds-as-feed-presence.md): the author feels a cloud as a gesture on their feed row, not as a list or count on the story page.
