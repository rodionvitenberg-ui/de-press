# ADR 0022: The therapy contour — a catalog, sessions with statuses, Solana Pay without processing

Date: 2026-08-30
Status: accepted

## Context

Some users ask for paid sessions with therapists. Introducing a full-blown
payment processing (acquiring, subscriptions, disputes, KYC) is something the
platform cannot and does not want to do: licenses, storing payment data, and a
new class of risks — while the platform is positioned as non-commercial and
anonymous (PRIVACY.md). At the same time there is a proven non-custodial pattern
(ADR-0020): money moves only wallet → wallet, the platform holds no keys. There
is also a live 1:1 session channel — a dialogue with text and a call (ADR-0021).

A therapist is not a Helper (ADR-0010): a different role, a different flow (an
admin invite, a rate, a payment confirmation), a separate surface.

## Decision

- **The therapist is a separate role by invite.** `TherapistProfile` with a
  one-time invite token generated by the Django admin; the token activates the
  profile via `POST /therapy/claim`. There is no open self-registration.
- **The backend — only statuses and references.** The `TherapySession` model
  stores the client, the therapist, the price and the status: `awaiting_payment →
  payment_claimed → paid → done`, plus `declined`. No amounts in flight, no
  balances, no charge attempts.
- **The payment — a direct client → therapist transfer** (Solana Pay: a
  `solana:<addr>?amount=…` deeplink + a QR; the address and the rate are public
  profile fields). The platform does not participate in the transfer and cannot
  verify it, so **the payment is confirmed manually by the therapist**
  (`payment_claimed` after "I paid" → `paid` by the therapist's decision;
  `declined` is possible). Idempotency: `confirm` from any "paying" status does
  not create a second entity.
- **Pseudonymity.** The client is visible to the therapist as `client_label` —
  their own optional pseudonym (or the generic "client"), not as an identity;
  `DialogueSource.THERAPY` marks the origin of the dialogue, the dialogue itself
  is a regular Initiated Dialogue with a call.
- **The surfaces.** A public catalog of active profiles
  (`GET /therapy/profiles`), a session request by an authorized client, the
  cabinets of both sides (`GET /me/therapy/sessions`, `GET /me/therapy/inbox`),
  the therapist actions `confirm|decline|complete`. The client can be anonymous
  (an anon cookie) — this is not revealed to the therapist beyond
  `client_label`.
- **ADR-0020 is untouched**: the fund, the tips and the duty live separately; the
  rate goes to the therapist, not the platform.

## Consequences

- +`apps/therapy` (models, services, an admin with an auto token), `api/v1/therapy.py`,
  migrations; `MeOut.is_therapist`; on the frontend — `features/therapy/`
  (TherapyPane: the catalog, the cabinets, the invite; PayModal: a QR + "I paid"),
  the `/therapy` route.
- The risk of "the client pressed I paid but did not transfer" is solved by a
  human: the therapist checks the arrival to their wallet and only then confirms.
  The platform is not a dispute arbiter — this is a deliberate limit of the seedling.
- There is no automatic reconciliation with the blockchain (phase 2): a payment
  signature/label, an address ownership verification — later, by need.
- The i18n catalog cap was raised 560 → 640 (`apps/common/i18n_ui.py`,
  `flatten.test.ts` synced).
